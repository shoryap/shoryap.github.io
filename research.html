Research

Research interests

My primary research interest is in operator algebras, with an emphasis on analytic and algebraic methods in the study of non-commutative structures.

I am also broadly interested in C*-algebras, commutative algebra, and representation theory of finite groups.

MS Thesis

Supervisor: Dr. Prahlad Vaidyanathan

During my MS Thesis at IISER Bhopal, I studied C*-algebras, group amenability, and group C*-algebras associated with the discrete Heisenberg group and the discrete free group on two generators, F2. The work included a verification of the Kadison–Kaplansky Conjecture for F2.
