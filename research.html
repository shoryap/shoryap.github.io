Research

Interests

My primary research interests lie in operator algebras, particularly the use of analytic and algebraic methods to study non-commutative structures.

My broader interests include commutative algebra and representation theory of finite groups.

MS Thesis

Supervisor: Dr. Prahlad Vaidyanathan

During my MS thesis at IISER Bhopal, I studied C*-algebras, group amenability, and group C*-algebras of the discrete Heisenberg group and the discrete free group on two generators F₂. I worked on verifying the Kadison–Kaplansky conjecture for F₂.
